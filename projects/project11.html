Three-Channel Power Supply

A regulated bench power supply with a variable 1.5V–24V channel plus fixed −12V and +5V channels, each using a different regulation technique.

Power Electronics Linear Regulation LM317

Abstract

This project involved designing and implementing a three-channel regulated DC power supply that converts AC mains voltage into three separate outputs: a variable 1.5 V to 24 V channel, a fixed −12 V channel, and a fixed +5 V channel. Each channel used a different regulation technique, providing outputs rated at 1 A, 0.5 A, and 0.5 A respectively.

Specifications

  • Variable output: 1.5 V – 24 V DC @ 1 A
  • Fixed output 1: −12 V DC @ 0.5 A
  • Fixed output 2: +5 V DC @ 0.5 A
  • AC input: 230 V mains

Variable 1.5V–24V Power Supply

Built around an LM317 adjustable regulator, fed from a 26 V transformer secondary through a bridge rectifier and 4700 µF / 100 µF filter capacitors, with a 4.7 kΩ potentiometer setting the output between 1.3 V (min) and 24.1 V (max). Output voltage and ripple capacitor sizing follow: 

Vout = 1.25 × (1 + R2 / R1)
C    = I / (2 × f × Vripple)

Fixed −12V Power Supply

  • Transformer: 12.1 V secondary
  • Bridge rectifier + 1000 µF filter capacitor
  • Zener diode: 12 V in reverse bias to generate the negative voltage reference
  • Pass transistor: TIP42 for current amplification
  • Output: −12 V regulated at 0.5 A

Fixed +5V Power Supply

  • Transformer: 13.1 V secondary
  • Bridge rectifier + 2200 µF filter capacitor
  • Zener diodes: dual 5.6 V zeners for a stable reference
  • Pass transistors: TIP41 pair for current handling
  • Output: 4.8 V measured (close to the theoretical 4.3 V)

Observations & Testing

Key parameters recorded for the variable supply:

  • Vrms: calculated 16.34 V, measured 19.4 V
  • Vdc: calculated 14.34 V, measured 16.5 V
  • Ripple factor (RFv): calculated 0.54, measured 0.618
  • Efficiency (η): calculated 0.771, measured 0.7233

Overall Simulations

The full system was simulated to validate regulation performance and ripple suppression. All three outputs operated within target specifications under load.

Conclusion

The three-channel power supply met all design objectives, producing regulated outputs of 1.3 V–24.1 V (variable), −12 V, and +5 V. Component choices such as the LM317, zener diodes, and pass transistors ensured stability and adequate current supply, with ripple minimized through appropriate capacitor sizing. The design is well-suited for lab bench applications requiring multiple DC voltage levels.
